Privacy Policy — JEIWS Mobile App

Last updated:

This policy covers the JEIWS mobile app (Admin CMS and Site Portal), used internally by J.E. Infrastructure Waterproofing & Services Pvt. Ltd. ("JEIWS", "we", "us") staff to manage construction projects, labour attendance, and materials stock. It is not a consumer app and is not intended for use by the general public.

Who this app is for

Access is restricted to JEIWS employees and authorized site staff, each given credentials directly by a JEIWS administrator. There is no public sign-up.

Information we collect

DataCollected fromPurpose
Username & password (hashed)Site staff accounts, set up by an adminSigning in to the Site Portal

Worker name, category, daily wage, phone (optional)Entered by admins/site staffMaintaining the labour roster for attendance tracking
Attendance records (date, status, project)Recorded by site staffPayroll and project labour tracking
Materials stock entriesRecorded by site staffTracking material usage per project
Project photosUploaded by adminsDocumenting project progress, shown on the public JEIWS website

We do not collect device location, contacts, browsing history, or advertising identifiers. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not sell or share data with third parties.

Device permissions

  • Internet access — required for every feature; the app is a client for our own server and does nothing offline.
  • Photo library access — only requested when an admin chooses to upload a project photo.

Where data is stored

All data is stored on JEIWS's own server and MySQL database, accessed over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. We do not use third-party cloud analytics, advertising, or data-broker services within the app.

Data retention

Attendance and stock records are retained for ongoing project and payroll record-keeping. Accounts and roster entries can be deactivated or removed by an administrator at any time; removing a worker or user from the system does not delete historical attendance/stock records already logged, which are kept for accounting purposes.

Your rights

Staff with an account can contact their administrator to review, correct, or request deletion of their personal information, subject to legitimate record-keeping requirements (e.g. payroll history).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
