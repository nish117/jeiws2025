    How to use the Naksa Plot Analyzer

    Four steps from upload to area.

    1. Upload Naapi Naksa — drag in a scanned/photographed cadastral map image (JPG, PNG or WEBP).
    2. Calibrate Scale — enter a real-world distance you know, then click its two endpoints on the map.
    3. Add Plot — click around a plot's corners. Every point can be dragged to fix it. Click near your first point (or press Finish) to close the shape and see its area — in sq.ft, sq.m, and Ropani/Aana/Bigha/Kattha. Hover any closed plot to see its area without selecting it.
    4. Measure Distance — a separate tool for checking the distance between any two points, without adding them to a plot.

    This tool gives an approximate digital measurement — it is not a substitute for an official government land survey.
